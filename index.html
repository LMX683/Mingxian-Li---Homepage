About Me

I am an undergraduate pursuing a B.Eng. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University in Shenzhen, China. At HCP Lab, Sun Yat-sen University, I am supervised by Xiaodan Liang and Xiao Dong.

My research interests are multimodal reasoning, 3D spatial intelligence, and AI for Science. I study how vision-language models connect 3D molecular geometry with chemical rules, with a focus on diagnosing reasoning failures and improving geometry-aware scientific reasoning.

My research includes:

  • 3D Molecular Reasoning: SeeChem, a diagnostic benchmark, and EasySeeChem-VLM, a geometry-enhanced vision-language model.
  • Geometric Reaction World Models: ReactWorld, exploring non-autoregressive models for condition-driven reaction product prediction and 3D transition generation.
  • Chemistry Benchmark and Data Synthesis: Sun Yat-sen University–Huawei collaboration on advanced university and competition chemistry.
