About Me

I am Mingxian Li, an undergraduate pursuing a B.Eng. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University in Shenzhen, China.

My research interests lie in multimodal reasoning, 3D spatial intelligence, and AI for Science. At the HCP Lab, I work with advisors Xiaodan Liang and Xiao Dong on diagnosing and improving how vision-language models connect 3D molecular geometry with chemical rules.

My current work includes SeeChem, a diagnostic benchmark for 3D chemical reasoning, and chemistry benchmark and data synthesis through a Sun Yat-sen University–Huawei collaboration.

Research Experience

SeeChem: 3D Molecular Reasoning in VLMs

Mar. 2026 – Present

Sun Yat-sen University, HCP Lab
Advisors: Xiaodan Liang, Xiao Dong

Diagnosing and improving how vision-language models connect 3D molecular geometry with chemical rules.

  • Developed SeeChem, a diagnostic benchmark with 5,065 questions across 320 molecular instances and five tasks, aligning spatial grounding, local rule application, and integrated reasoning within shared molecular contexts.
  • Constructed SeeChem-Data with verifiable supervision across three reasoning stages, using molecular instances excluded from the benchmark.
  • Developed EasySeeChem-VLM, integrating Uni-Mol2 geometry representations into Qwen3-VL-8B through a gated projector; evaluated the method alongside nine external VLMs with task- and stage-specific analysis.

Manuscript under review at AAAI 2027; advanced to Phase 2 review.
